Reading Buddhist anarchism

November 15th 2023

Gary Snyder and Stephen Batchelor gave me ways to connect Buddhist practice with questions about autonomy and how we live with other people.

I keep coming back to impermanence and attention. They give me a way to notice when I’m treating my own habits as inevitable, and to ask how I could act differently with the people around me.

  • Gary Snyder
  • Stephen Batchelor
