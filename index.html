Hi, I’m Sebastián.

If we unfolded a map between us, I’d probably trace a current out to sea. You might put your finger on a stretch of coast you know by heart. I’d love to hear what the map leaves out.
That curiosity follows me to the keyboard, into the classroom and, every so often, up to an open mic. Make yourself at home.

Analytical & Creative Skills

When I work with data, I tend to draw while I’m thinking. A sketch can make a pattern obvious, or show me exactly where I’ve stopped understanding it.

How I work with others

A half-formed question is plenty to bring to a first conversation. I’d like to hear what’s been puzzling you and what you’ve already tried.
Frame the question

>_Getting the question onto one sheet of paper gives everyone a chance to cross something out or add the bit we’ve missed.

Get the data

>_If collecting the files takes an afternoon, a small tool can do the fetching. It can remember the sources and dates for the next person, too.

Check the evidence

>_Set a prediction beside an observation the model has never seen. Where they disagree, there’s usually something worth looking into.

Make it runnable

>_Pack the code and its settings together so a colleague can open the same workspace on another machine and rerun the analysis.

Trade notes

>_In a class or a workshop, give everyone the same figure and a minute to look. Hearing what each person noticed is part of the lesson.

Hand it over

>_Let the person who’ll use the tool take the controls. Their first few questions usually tell us what the instructions are missing.
