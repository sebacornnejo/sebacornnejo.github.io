>_PEER-REVIEWED PUBLICATIONS

Biogeography of living coccolithophores in the Southeast Pacific: patterns and drivers of Gephyrocapsa spp. diversity

2026 · Progress in Oceanography. With Francisco Díaz-Rosas and colleagues, we used field sampling and satellite observations to examine how upwelling and environmental gradients shape coccolithophore diversity.

On the Relationship Between Atmospheric Events of the Von Kármán Streets in the Robinson Crusoe Island and South Pacific Climate Variability

2025 · International Journal of Climatology. With Constanza Amaya-Cárcamo and colleagues, we examined when atmospheric vortex streets form in the lee of Robinson Crusoe Island and how they relate to South Pacific climate variability.

Population genomic and biophysical modeling show different patterns of population connectivity in the spiny lobster Jasus frontalis inhabiting oceanic islands

2024 · Marine Environmental Research. I used monthly climatologies to identify where particles released around the islands are likely to be retained or transported between sites.

Diversity and toxicity of the planktonic diatom genus Pseudo-nitzschia from coastal and offshore waters of the Southeast Pacific, including Pseudo-nitzschia dampieri sp. nov.

2023 · Harmful Algae. I used satellite chlorophyll-a and geostrophic velocities to describe the ocean conditions during sampling off Chile.

Petrale sole transboundary connectivity and settlement success: a biophysical approach

2023 · Frontiers in Marine Science. I interpreted results from individual-based simulations of petrale sole by examining circulation in the California Current System.

High levels of connectivity over large distances in the diadematid sea urchin Centrostephanus sylviae

2021 · PLOS ONE. I analysed how circulation between remote island systems changes over time and what that means for sea urchin connectivity.

Local and remote physical processes driving variability of the planktonic system in the Juan Fernández Archipelago: A multidisciplinary framework providing conservation insights

2021 · Aquatic Conservation: Marine and Freshwater Ecosystems. I examined local and remote atmospheric and oceanographic processes associated with changes in plankton around the Juan Fernández Archipelago.

SWOT Cal/Val in the Argentine Continental Shelf

Satellite altimetry collaboration with LEGOS
