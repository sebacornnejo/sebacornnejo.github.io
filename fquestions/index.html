Frequently Asked Questions

Working together on research and data projects

Can you work with clients remotely? [+]
Yes. I’ve worked remotely with teams in different countries. Email and shared documents usually handle the day-to-day work, with calls when a discussion helps.
How long does it typically take to complete a research or data science project? [+]
It depends on the question, the state of the data and the deliverables. Once we’ve looked at the inputs, we can agree on a timeline and the first milestone.
Do you offer coding support and maintenance services? [+]
Yes. We can agree on maintenance, documentation and handover as part of the project.
Can you automate or optimize my data analysis codes? [+]
Yes. I can review an existing analysis, identify slow or repetitive steps and turn them into a reproducible workflow.
Can you integrate third-party tools, platforms and databases into my project? [+]
Yes. We can review the systems and access you already have, then decide how to connect them and document the setup.
