ACADEMIC PORTFOLIO/Reports and gray literature

Marine spatial planning in Chilean Patagonia

2023 · Consultancy. Work on the coastal and marine uses of Chilean Patagonia as inputs for marine spatial planning.

Physical and biogeochemical characterization of periods with and without eddies off Valparaíso (~33ºS) - Matías Castillo Thesis

I served as an external adviser and committee member for Matías Castillo’s undergraduate thesis and supported the identification and tracking of mesoscale eddies.

Fishing biological monitoring of fisheries and the ecosystem associated with the Juan Fernández Archipelago, 2021

I contributed to the annual report on the ocean conditions around the islands, including indicators relevant to lobster and sea urchin.

Crustacean fishery in the Juan Fernández Archipelago, 2019

We monitored conditions around Robinson Crusoe and Santa Clara with instruments in the water and used satellite observations for Alejandro Selkirk and the Desventuradas Islands.

Crustacean fishery in the Juan Fernández Archipelago, 2018

I worked with researchers and island residents to examine variability in environmental conditions relevant to the Juan Fernández crustacean fishery.

Environmental effects on the capture rates of Jasus frontalis in the Juan Fernández Archipelago and Desventuradas Islands in the 2006-2017 seasons

I helped compile environmental indicators for a statistical model of lobster catches around Juan Fernández and the Desventuradas Islands.

Annual and interannual variability of the number of submesoscale fronts and its connection with the chlorophyll increases of Desventuradas Islands and the Juan Fernández Archipelago

We filled gaps in satellite chlorophyll-a with DINEOF, identified fronts and analysed their seasonal and interannual variability around Juan Fernández and the Desventuradas Islands.

Mesoscale Mechanisms and Biogeochemical Variables That Determine the Variability of Chlorophyll-a in the Juan Fernández Archipelago - Thesis

My undergraduate thesis compared mesoscale eddies with other processes that may explain chlorophyll-a variability around Juan Fernández.

Meteorological structure and physical-biogeochemical characteristics of the internal waters of the Madre de Dios Archipelago: A refuge from acidification in Southern Chile

We used observations from the 2015 campaign to describe day-to-day weather, precipitation and the hydrography of the archipelago, and to explore links with coccolithophores.

Quantification of chlorophyll-a structures coupled to submesoscale frontal regions off Central Chile

We compared a front index with satellite chlorophyll-a at about 1 km resolution to see where the two fields coincide off central Chile.

Mesoscale mechanisms and biogeochemical variables that determine the variability of chlorophyll-a in the Juan Fernández Archipelago: Part I

We examined whether changes in mesoscale circulation coincide with the transport of waters with different biogeochemical properties from coastal Chile to Juan Fernández.

Hydrodynamic Model for the Arauco Gulf and Chiloé Inland Sea(Chile)

I co-authored a conference study applying ROMS-AGRIF to south-central Chile to examine circulation variability in the Arauco Gulf and Chiloé Inner Sea using high-resolution wind forcing.

Detection, characterization and analysis of mesoscale eddies over the Eastern South Pacific, and their influence on Challenger Break

We tracked mesoscale eddies in the eastern South Pacific, including the Challenger Break and the East Pacific ridge. Eddy activity was low in the study area used for jack mackerel spawning during the period we examined.

High-resolution hydrodynamic modeling using ROMS, in areas of interest for mussel farming within the Chiloé Inner Sea

My first multi-year ROMS simulation examined how tides and discharge from the Puelo River shape circulation in the Chiloé Inner Sea. The model reproduced key circulation features and underestimated temperature and salinity.
