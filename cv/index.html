Sebastián E. Cornejo-Guzmán

Physical oceanographer · Geophysicist · Data scientist · Buenos Aires, Argentina

Research and teaching

  • Doctoral researcher and CONICET fellow at CIMA (CONICET–UBA), working on fine-scale ocean dynamics and ecosystem connectivity.
  • Instructor and coordinator of practical oceanography classes, Department of Atmospheric and Ocean Sciences, Universidad de Buenos Aires.
  • Geophysics degree, Universidad de Concepción, Chile, 2018.
  • JICMar coordination team since late 2024.
  • Research stay at LEGOS, Toulouse, 2026. IFAECI announcement.
